About The Book

Every woman you admire has a story you may never have seen. Before I Became Her is more than a memoir—it is a glimpse into the journey behind the woman. A journey marked by pain, difficult seasons, faith, resilience, and the courage to keep moving when life became difficult. Behind the smile was a story. Behind the strength were moments that tested her. And somewhere between what she endured and what she believed, she discovered the woman she was becoming. This book invites you to look beyond the finished picture and remember that becoming is a process. Sometimes, the strongest version of us is built from the very moments we thought would break us.
